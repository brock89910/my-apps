Our Story

Moncton's teppanyaki table

Osaka Hibachi brings the theatre of teppanyaki to the heart of Moncton. Our chefs cook at your table on open iron grills — AAA steaks, fresh seafood, and vibrant vegetables prepared with skill and a touch of showmanship.

Beyond the hibachi, our kitchen crafts traditional Japanese and Korean dishes: handmade sushi rolls, premium sashimi, and a curated sake selection. Every meal here is an occasion.
